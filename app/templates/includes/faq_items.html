How do I book a session?

Simply click the "Book Now" button, select your preferred package, choose a date and location, and complete the payment. You'll receive instant confirmation!

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 24 hours in advance. However, you can book up to 90 days ahead for peak season dates.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes! You can cancel or reschedule up to 24 hours before your session for a full refund.

What equipment do you use?

We use professional 4K cameras, drones, GoPros, and stabilization equipment to capture the best angles and quality footage.

How many runs will you film?

As many as your package allows! We'll follow you for the duration of your booked session, capturing multiple runs and angles.

Do you provide drone footage?

Drone footage is included in our Pro and Epic packages! It adds stunning aerial perspectives to your videos.

How long until I receive my videos?

Edited videos are delivered within 48-72 hours. Raw footage is available immediately after your session.

What format will my videos be in?

Videos are delivered in MP4 format (1080p or 4K) optimized for social media sharing, plus downloadable high-quality versions.

Can I request revisions?

Absolutely! We offer one round of revisions for all packages to ensure you're completely satisfied.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards through our secure Stripe payment system.

Is my payment information secure?

Yes! All payments are processed securely through Stripe with SSL encryption. We never store your credit card information.

Can you film groups?

Yes! Most packages allow for multiple riders. We can capture your entire group's adventure together.

Do you work with all skill levels?

Absolutely! From first-timers to pros, we tailor our filming and editing to showcase your unique style and skill level.
